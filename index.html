NavCalc
Aviation · UtilitiesiOS · Android · AppGallery

A precise navigation calculator for pilots and aviation students — unit conversions, geodesy, wind triangles, ISA and a complete aviation reference in one calm interface.

Download on theApp StoreGet it onGoogle PlayExplore it onAppGallery
Overview SupportPrivacy Policy
  • 01Unit conversionsDistance, height, speed, time, pressure, temperature and more — switch between unit systems instantly, without ambiguity.
  • 02GeodesyDirect and inverse geodetic problems, magnetic variation for any location, intersection of two radials and distance to the horizon.
  • 03Flight tasksWind triangle, crosswind and headwind components, holding pattern entries and parameters of turn.
  • 04AtmosphereISA values at altitude, TAS from IAS, QFE ⇄ QNH conversion and cold-temperature altimeter corrections.

Education · AviationiOS · Android

02 / Open source

The maths behind the apps, shared.

The engineering core of my aviation apps is published as open-source packages for the Dart & Flutter community.

isa
pub.dev

International Standard Atmosphere for Dart & Flutter. Physical characteristics of a standard or customised atmosphere at any altitude up to 80 km, according to ICAO Doc 7488.

$flutter pub add isa
measures
pub.dev

Type-safe units of measure. Comfortable handling of everyday units, seamless conversions and code that is unambiguous about what a number means.

$flutter pub add measures
