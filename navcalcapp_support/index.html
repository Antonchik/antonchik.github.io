01 / Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

01

Unit conversions

Distance, height, speed, time, pressure, temperature and more — switch between unit systems instantly, without ambiguity.

02

Geodesy

Direct and inverse geodetic problems, magnetic variation for any location, intersection of two radials and distance to the horizon.

03

Flight tasks

Wind triangle, crosswind and headwind components, holding pattern entries and parameters of turn.

04

Atmosphere

ISA values at altitude, TAS from IAS, QFE ⇄ QNH conversion and cold-temperature altimeter corrections.

05

Reference

Aircraft, airline and airport data, the phonetic alphabet with Morse code and a large handbook of aviation abbreviations.

06

AIRAC calendar

Always know when the next AIRAC cycle becomes effective — and the dates of the ones after it.

FAQ

Questions about NavCalc

Are the calculations suitable for real flight planning?

NavCalc is built carefully against published standards (including ICAO Doc 7488 for the atmosphere model), but it is a training and reference aid. Always verify results against approved sources and your aircraft documentation.

Which standards does NavCalc use?

All common aviation units are supported. Atmosphere calculations follow the ICAO Standard Atmosphere (Doc 7488).

I found a wrong value or a missing abbreviation.

Thank you — please email the details (what you entered, what you expected, and your app version) and it will be corrected in a future update.

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address of the app with a short description, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, OS version and app version. Screenshots help a lot.
