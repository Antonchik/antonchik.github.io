FAQ

Questions about Pilotiq

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pilotiq → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pilotiq → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe, open Pilotiq and use “Restore purchases”. If it does not work, email support with the date of purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or through your Google Play order history.

I think an answer in the question bank is wrong.

Please email the question text and the answer you believe is correct — every report is reviewed.

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address of the app with a short description, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, OS version and app version. Screenshots help a lot.
