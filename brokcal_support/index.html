FAQ

Questions about Brokcal

Where is my data stored?

Your diary, products and goals are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Is Brokcal medical advice?

No. Brokcal provides general estimates for informational purposes only. Consult a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet.

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address of the app with a short description, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, OS version and app version. Screenshots help a lot.
