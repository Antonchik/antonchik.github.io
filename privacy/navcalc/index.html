In short: NavCalc works without an account, keeps your data on your device, and never sells personal information. This page explains the details.

01Who we are

NavCalc (the “App”) is developed and published by Anton Antonchik, an independent developer (“I”, “we”, “us”). I am the controller of any personal data processed in connection with the App. You can reach me at any time at navcalc@antonchik.com.

02Summary

  • The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Data you enter in the App is stored locally on your device.
  • I do not sell your personal data.
  • Limited technical data may be processed by the third-party services listed below.

03Information we process

Data stored on your device

Settings, preferences and content you create in the App are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to me.

Technical and diagnostic data

To keep the App stable, anonymous diagnostic information may be collected automatically: device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs and general usage events. This information does not identify you personally.

Advertising data

If the App shows ads, the advertising partner may process your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and approximate (not precise) location. On iOS you will be asked for permission before any tracking, via App Tracking Transparency.

Support correspondence

If you email me, I receive your email address and the contents of your message. I use them only to answer you.

04How and why we use data

  • To provide the App and its features (performance of a contract).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the App (legitimate interest).
  • To show advertising that funds the free version (consent, where required by law).
  • To reply to your support requests (legitimate interest).

06Data retention

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Diagnostic data is retained by the providers for the periods set out in their policies (typically up to 90 days for crash reports). Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and then deleted.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email navcalc@antonchik.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

08Deleting your data

  • In-app data: uninstall the App — all locally stored data is removed.
  • Support emails: write to navcalc@antonchik.com with the subject “Data deletion”. Your correspondence will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Advertising identifier: reset or limit it in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

09Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please get in touch and it will be deleted.

10Security

I use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. Data handled by third-party providers is protected by their own security programmes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

11International transfers

Third-party providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

12Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted in the App or on this page.

13Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: navcalc@antonchik.com.
