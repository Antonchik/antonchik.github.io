This policy explains what data the NavCalc app for Android, iOS and the web (navcalc-app.web.app) handles, and why. NavCalc is published by Anton Antonchik, who is responsible for the app (the "data controller"). Contact: navcalc@antonchik.com.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app does not use your device's location (GPS).
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows no ads.
  • If you buy NavCalc Pro on Android or iOS, RevenueCat checks the purchase. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your NavCalc purchase history, not your name, email address or payment card.
  • Coordinates you type into the elevation screen are sent to GeoNames to get the result. Airport codes can be sent to aviationweather.gov for the weather and airport screens.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings, such as language, dark mode and the units you chose, in local storage on your device (in your browser for the web version). This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, removes it.

With NavCalc Pro, the app also keeps on your device the METAR reports of the aerodromes you watch or recently looked up on the METAR screen, for 12, 24 or 48 hours (your choice in Settings, 24 hours by default) and up to 10 MB in total; above that, the oldest reports are deleted first. It also keeps the runways and frequencies last received for each airport card you opened, so they can be shown when they cannot be loaded. These copies have no time limit: they stay until you clear them in Settings or delete the app.

With NavCalc Pro, the app also keeps on your device the list of the aerodrome codes you recently looked up on the METAR screen (the last 5, 10 or 20, your choice in Settings). You can remove a code from this list on the METAR screen; deleting the app removes the whole list.

This data is public aeronautical information. It stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete the stored reports and airport copies at any time in Settings with Clear.

All calculations run on your device. The values you enter in them are not sent anywhere, except for the online features below.

Online features: elevation, METAR and airports

The Point elevation screen sends the coordinates you enter to the GeoNames web service (geonames.org) over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. GeoNames returns the elevation. See the GeoNames website for how GeoNames handles data.

The METAR screen and the airport card get weather data from aviationweather.gov (the US Aviation Weather Center), over HTTPS. The app downloads the complete METAR and TAF data files, which do not contain your airport code, and looks the airport up on your device. The files are saved in the app's storage on your device and reused until they are older than the age you choose in Settings; like your settings, they are removed when you delete the app. The airport code (ICAO) is sent to aviationweather.gov each time you look up an airport on the METAR screen while you are online, to load its earlier reports, and each time you open an airport card with an ICAO code, to load its runways and frequencies. With NavCalc Pro you can turn on background refresh in Settings. Then, when the system allows, the app sends the ICAO codes of your watched aerodromes to aviationweather.gov in one request and keeps the reports on your device. It is off by default and uses no location or notifications.

As with any web request, GeoNames and aviationweather.gov also receive your IP address. Requests are made only when you use these screens or have turned on background refresh.

Purchases: NavCalc Pro

NavCalc Pro is a one-time purchase in the Android and iOS apps. The payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment card details. To check and restore the purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc. (revenuecat.com/privacy), which processes this data on our behalf:

  • an anonymous app user ID that the app creates at its first start (it is not your store account, name or email address);
  • your NavCalc purchase history: the product, its price and currency, the date, the store's transaction identifiers and the store country;
  • technical details needed to talk to the stores: the app version, the operating system and its version, the device's language, and your IP address.

This data is used only to provide NavCalc Pro: to unlock it, to restore it on another device and to follow refunds. It is not used for marketing or tracking, not linked to other apps or websites, not sold, and it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The app also keeps on your device whether Pro is active, so that Pro works offline. The app also reads the country of your store account from the store through RevenueCat, only to make Pro free in countries where the store cannot take payments; the result is kept on your device. To have the data held by RevenueCat deleted, write to us at navcalc@antonchik.com (the purchase itself stays with your store account and can be restored). The web version has no purchases.

Web version

The web version is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, and the browser loads part of the app's rendering engine from Google's servers (www.gstatic.com). Like any website, these servers receive your IP address and browser details to deliver the files. The web version uses no cookies and no analytics.

Legal basis

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies: the elevation, METAR and airport requests are made to provide the feature you asked for, and the purchase data is processed to perform the purchase you made; delivering the web version's files relies on our legitimate interest in running the app.

Children

NavCalc is intended for pilots, students and other aviation users. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers, because the app has none; RevenueCat holds the purchase data above on our behalf. For data processed by Google (web hosting and Google Play), Apple (the App Store), GeoNames or aviationweather.gov, you can contact them directly, or contact us at navcalc@antonchik.com and we will help. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
