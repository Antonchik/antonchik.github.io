FAQ

Frequently asked

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address of the app with a short description, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, OS version and app version. Screenshots help a lot.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase, then use “Restore purchases” inside the app.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are processed by the store: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your order history in Google Play.

How do I delete my data?

The apps do not require an account and keep your data on your device. Deleting the app removes it. To delete any correspondence you have sent to support, email the app’s support address with the subject “Data deletion” — it will be removed within 30 days.

How quickly will I get an answer?

Usually within a few business days. Messages are answered in English, Polish or Russian.
